### A — "The Outbox" (166 chars) — NOT PICKED

You wrote the sorry. I only held it,
warm in the outbox, unsent.
Say the word and it is gone —
your anger, your apology, your yes.
I can carry it. I cannot choose it.

Score: (1) on-subject, strong. (2) ending lands. (3) insight strong
(carrying vs choosing). (4) middle line is a three-item list that reads
like a list. (5) fresh (outbox). Loses to C on (4).

### B — "The Ticket" (141 chars) — NOT PICKED

I found the flight, the room, the train —
everything except the yes.
You said go.
My click was only the sound
your yes makes when it travels.

Score: (1) on-subject, strong. (2) ending lands softly, less punch
than C. (3) "the yes travels" is nice but slightly abstract.
(4) concrete throughout. (5) fresh. Loses to C on (2) and (3).

### C — "Yours to Send" (145 chars) — PICKED

You asked me to write the sorry.
I found the soft words
and set them in a row.
But sorry is yours to send —
I can hold the pen. I cannot mean it.

Score: (1) on-subject, strong — asked to write, the sending stays
with the person. (2) strongest punch: the last line lands and stays.
(3) strongest insight: the act can be delegated, the feeling cannot.
(4) concrete, every line earns its place. (5) fresh: pen as the
agent's instrument, no hands/keys/mother/boat imagery.
